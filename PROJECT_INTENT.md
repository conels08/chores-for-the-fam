# Project Intent: ChoreSpace

## Purpose
ChoreSpace is a family chores and tasks web application designed to help households stay organized, accountable, and fair. It provides a shared “chore-space” where family members can see assigned chores, complete them, and earn points. The system supports real-life family dynamics by keeping roles clear, chores visible, and rewards consistent.

## Core Goals
- Create a shared family space (“chore-space”) where all members participate.
- Make it easy for admins (parents/guardians) to assign chores and track completion.
- Encourage accountability and motivation through a transparent point system.
- Ensure each family member has their own login so data stays personal and accurate.
- Provide a shared family calendar for scheduling and visibility.
- Keep chores, calendar, and to-do lists distinct but connected when needed.

## Role Model
- **Admin (Parent/Guardian)**
  - The first person to sign up becomes the admin by default.
  - Can invite other members to the same chore-space.
  - Can assign chores and tasks to members.
  - Can set member tiers, except when the invited user is designated as a child.
- **Child**
  - Cannot be promoted to admin.
  - Can view and complete assigned chores.
  - Earns points for completed tasks.
- **Member (Non-Admin)**
  - Can view and complete assigned chores.
  - Earns points for completed tasks.

## Invitations & Family Setup
- The admin creates the family account and establishes the chore-space.
- The admin sends invitations to family members.
- Invitation flow supports identifying a member as a child or non-child.
- Admins can assign roles for non-children, but child accounts cannot become admins.

## Chores, Tasks, and Points
- Admins create chores or tasks and assign them to members.
- Non-admins can check off assigned chores/tasks.
- The system records completion and awards points.
- Points are visible to encourage consistency and progress.
- Deadlines can be added to chores or tasks and optionally displayed on the calendar.

## Calendar
- A shared family calendar shows the same schedule to all members.
- The calendar auto-follows the current date like a standard calendar.
- Admins manage calendar events.
- Chores can be optionally shown as calendar items, but the calendar can operate independently.

## To-Do Lists
- A separate to-do area exists apart from chores and the calendar.
- To-do items can have deadlines, and those deadlines can optionally appear on the calendar.

## Family Menu
- Admins can post and update a family menu (meals and food plans).
- Non-admins can view the menu but cannot edit it.

## Real-Life Dynamics This App Supports
- **Clarity:** Everyone knows what’s expected of them and what’s already done.
- **Fairness:** Work distribution is visible, helping reduce conflict.
- **Motivation:** Points help reinforce positive habits.
- **Flexibility:** Families can adjust tasks, points, and roles over time.

## Guiding Principles
- Keep setup simple so families can get started quickly.
- Prioritize clarity and trust in family relationships.
- Make the system feel supportive, not punitive.
